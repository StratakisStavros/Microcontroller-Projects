# Third-party libraries and assets

The design uses symbols, footprints and 3D models from KiCad's standard libraries. The two footprints in `hardware/Board_Local.pretty` are project-local variants of the KiCad USB connector and PCM12 slide-switch footprints. Their upstream descriptions are retained in the files. See [KiCad library licensing](https://www.kicad.org/libraries/license/) for the terms that apply to upstream library assets.

`hardware/3dmodels/629105150521.stp` is the supplied Würth Elektronik 629105150521 USB connector model, retained for portable mechanical visualization. Manufacturer rights and applicable model terms remain with their respective owners; it is not presented as a newly authored model.

The repository's existing Arduino software licensing statement does not relicense third-party CAD assets or establish a separate open-hardware licence for this board.
